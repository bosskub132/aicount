# AICount Project Vision

## Goal
AICount is a **full replacement for Express Accounting software**, not just a document processing tool that exports to Express. It should handle the complete accounting loop natively.

## Target Users
Professional accountants and bookkeepers at Thai SMEs who use the app daily. Power users who value efficiency, keyboard shortcuts, and information density.

## Design Direction
- **Style:** Xero (clean, trustworthy) meets Notion (information density, workspace feel)
- **Palette:** Trust Blue — primary #2563EB, success #059669, destructive #DC2626
- **Typography:** Inter + Noto Sans Thai (replacing Geist Sans), Geist Mono for monospace
- **App Shell:** Light white sidebar (230px) with grouped navigation, sticky header

## Full Accounting Modules (planned)
1. Document Intake (OCR + AI) — existing
2. General Ledger — journal entries, trial balance
3. Accounts Receivable — invoices, receipts, aging
4. Accounts Payable — purchase orders, payments, aging
5. Tax & WHT — ภ.พ.30, ภ.พ.36, ภ.ง.ด. series, 50 ทวิ
6. Financial Statements — Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow
7. Bank Reconciliation — matching, imports
8. Settings & Master Data — COA, vendors, customers, departments

## Subscription Model
Three tiers: Starter (฿499/mo), Professional (฿1,499/mo), Business (฿3,999/mo).
Core accounting features (reports, export, tax) available in ALL tiers.
Scaling axes: tenant count, users, document volume, AI intelligence level, automation depth.
