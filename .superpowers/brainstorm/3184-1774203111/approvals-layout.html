A. Queue-Based

One at a time, full screen. Progress bar shows position. "Approve & Next" auto-advances. Like processing a stack of papers.

Pros

  • Focused, fast processing
  • Clear progress tracking
  • Keyboard shortcuts friendly

Cons

  • Can't jump to specific doc
  • Less context of full queue

B. Split View

Queue list on left, selected document detail on right. Click any doc to review. Actions stay in-context.

Pros

  • See full queue at glance
  • Jump to any document
  • Familiar email-client UX

Cons

  • Less preview space
  • More visual complexity

C. Inline in Documents

No separate Approvals page. "Approvals" nav pre-filters Documents to "Pending" tab. Side panel has approve/reject actions.

Pros

  • Single source of truth
  • No page duplication
  • Consistent UX everywhere

Cons

  • Side panel space limited
  • Less focused than dedicated page
